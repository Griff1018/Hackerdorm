## 2. Interview questions prepared (to be used only if the opportunity appears)

1. What do you sell, and what records do you keep today — files, apps, paper?
2. How long does it take you to work out what to buy or restock? What do you look at?
3. When did you last run out of something, or find out a product was not worth making?
4. Where do you look for suppliers, and how long does that take?
5. Would you let an agent prepare a purchase order for you if you still pressed "pay"?
6. What would make you stop using a tool like this?
